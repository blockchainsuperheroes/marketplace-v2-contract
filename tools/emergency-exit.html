Emergency exit: take an NFT out of a Pentagon Prize Lock

Works without the Redeem website, its server or the keeper. It talks straight to the lock on Ethereum mainnet through your wallet (MetaMask etc.). It needs two wallets: the lock admin (0xB2e3…EF61f) signs an approval (free), and the depositor (the wallet that checked the NFT in) sends the withdraw. The lock only ever returns an NFT to its depositor. Open this file from your disk, or serve it with npx serve if your wallet ignores file:// pages.

Before withdrawing a listed piece, delist it on Pentagon Chain (see EMERGENCY-EXIT.md), otherwise someone could still claim the listing and would have to reclaim their payment.
